Fluffy Homemade Omurice

By Hana ★ 4.8

A comforting Japanese classic made with flavorful fried rice wrapped in a soft, fluffy omelette. Perfect for a cozy homemade meal!

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 bowl of cooked rice
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 100g chicken, cut into small pieces
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the ingredients. Chop the onion and chicken into small pieces.
  2. Cook the rice. Heat oil in a pan and cook the chicken and onion. Add the rice and ketchup, then mix well.
  3. Make the omelette. Beat the eggs and cook them in a lightly oiled pan until soft and fluffy.
  4. Put it together. Place the fried rice on a plate and cover it with the omelette. Add ketchup if desired.
